## Delivered behavior

- Campaigns now exposes readable requirements, whole-plan execution, clone, amendments, observed-state acceptance
  and journal/recovery over the existing reviewed engine.
- Home and seven primary navigation destinations connect plans, creative, report definitions/snapshots, schedules,
  client playbooks, capability discovery and operations. Exact links, identity-bound state and late-response guards
  keep work in the correct session and page.
- Reports have durable aggregate collection budgets, fair continuation, native empty/null corrections, reconciled X
  hourly boundaries, guided detailed capture, retained-evidence analysis, read-only monitoring and client report packs.
- Client playbooks preview names, tracking and templates, enforce their selected account bindings and preserve
  revisions. Creative matrices can become resumable media plans with per-item original source hashes and receipts.
- Google inventory, GTM configuration diagnosis and Meta product ingestion expose bounded new website workflows
  backed by existing native contracts. Illustrative budget scenarios preserve their data and assumptions.
- Privacy, dispatch authority, source finalization, structured errors, worker diagnostics and dependency advisories
  are addressed with targeted regressions. Native family classification and existing owner controls are preserved.
