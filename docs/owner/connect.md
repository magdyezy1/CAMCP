# Connect your accounts

AMCP works only with the platforms you connect and the accounts you select. Owners and administrators manage
connections.

<a id="connect-a-platform"></a><a id="choose-what-amcp-may-use"></a>

1. In **Connections**, choose **Connect** on a platform's card, sign in on the platform's page and approve the access.
   You return to **Connection result**.
2. Choose **Manage**, tick the accounts AMCP may use and choose **Save selection**, or **Save and check access** to
   read each new account once.
3. Under **Resource write access**, tick the accounts that allow reviewed changes and choose **Save write access**.
   The others stay read-only.

Saving changes nothing on the platform. A card that says *Administrator setup required* needs the platform's app first
([what stays with you](owner-run.md)).

## Keep connections working

<a id="several-logins"></a>

- **+ Connect another** adds a login; **Make default** tells AI assistants which one to use.
- **Reauthorize** renews a login and keeps its work. Use it when a card says *Reauthorization required* or lists
  *Declined permissions*.
- **Disconnect** removes the login's credentials at once. Revoke AMCP inside the platform too.

<a id="ai-assistants-optional"></a>

**AI connectors** connects Claude, ChatGPT, Codex, Cursor or another MCP client. Each person connects their own, within
their role, and can choose read-only access.
