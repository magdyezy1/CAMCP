# Privacy and retention

<a id="your-part"></a>

AMCP is designed around the GDPR and Saudi Arabia's PDPL. This page is not legal advice: confirm your obligations with
your counsel. The public [Privacy](https://amcp.shop/privacy) page explains it.

<a id="what-amcp-keeps"></a><a id="how-long"></a>

| What | Kept |
|---|---|
| Files uploaded from your computer | 30 minutes |
| Access requests never approved | 30 days |
| Accounts whose access was removed | 90 days |
| Finished report records | Your period in **Workspace settings → Retention**, otherwise 365 days |
| Audit history and change receipts | Kept, so the record stays complete |

<a id="personal-data-in-advertising"></a>

AMCP never reads submitted leads. Customer lists are hashed before they leave AMCP, only on accounts where the owner
allowed personal data.

## Deleting data

- **Disconnect** removes a login's credentials; revoke AMCP at the platform too.
- **Delete your account**, in **Your account**, erases your own workspace and gives you a receipt.
- Others write to privacy@amcp.shop from their account's address.

Deletion is immediate in AMCP's database; older backups expire on their own schedule. Downloads and Google Sheets
copies are yours to delete.
