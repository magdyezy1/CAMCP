# Privacy and retention

AMCP is designed around the EU's General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and Saudi Arabia's Personal Data Protection
Law (PDPL). This page describes what AMCP does; it is not legal advice. What you must do depends on your clients and
the countries you serve, so confirm it with your counsel. The public [Privacy](https://amcp.shop/privacy),
[Terms](https://amcp.shop/terms) and [Data deletion](https://amcp.shop/data-deletion) pages, in English and Arabic,
are what your team, visitors and platform reviewers read.

## What AMCP keeps

- The people of each workspace, their roles, and an audit history of what each one did.
- Platform logins, encrypted, and the accounts you selected.
- Plans, reviews and their receipts, saved reports and their snapshots, schedules and delivery records.
- Files uploaded from your computer, for a short time.

Reports show lead counts, but AMCP never reads the leads themselves: a request for submitted leads is refused.

## How long

These are the defaults; your operator can change them.

| What | Kept |
|---|---|
| Files uploaded from your computer | 30 minutes after the upload starts |
| Access requests never approved | Erased after 30 days |
| Accounts whose access was removed | Erased 90 days later |
| Finished report records | For your workspace's period, 7 to 365 days, set in **Workspace settings → Retention**; otherwise 365 days |
| Archived campaign plans | Until a period you set deletes them |
| Audit history and change receipts | Kept, so that the record of what changed stays complete |

Saved reports, settings, export destinations and schedules stay until they, or the workspace, are deleted.

## Deleting data

- **Disconnect a login** (Connections → Manage → Disconnect): its credentials, account list and selections are
  removed at once. Revoke AMCP at the platform too.
- **Delete your account** (Your account → Delete your account): review what will be deleted, type your email address
  to confirm, and keep the erasure receipt. Your own workspace and everything in it are erased; if other members use
  that workspace, remove them first.
- **A whole workspace, or someone who cannot sign in**: they write to privacy@amcp.shop from the email address on
  their AMCP account, or ask you.

A deletion removes the data from AMCP's live database at once. Older backups and storage snapshots are not rewritten:
they leave when their own retention ends, and the deletion is applied again to any copy that is restored. This is
retention by the application, not instant erasure from every copy, and the public pages say so. Keep backup retention
short ([what stays with you](owner-run.md)).

## Personal data in advertising

- **Customer lists and offline conversions** are sent only through reviewed changes, and only on accounts where the
  owner turned personal data on (each account starts off). Every upload needs the owner's approval when it runs. The
  values are hashed in memory before they leave AMCP, and nothing about the people is stored: only counts.
- **Viewers** cannot download report files unless you allow it.
- **Copies outside AMCP**, such as downloaded CSV files and reports written to Google Sheets, are yours to share and
  delete. Deleting data in AMCP does not delete anything inside the advertising platforms.

## Your part

As the workspace owner you decide who has access, which accounts are connected, which accounts may receive personal
data, and how long reports are kept. Requests from your clients' customers about data held in an advertising platform
go to that platform; for data held in AMCP, use the steps above or write to privacy@amcp.shop.
