# Launch a paused campaign through review

The guided workflows on **Campaigns** take a brief to a paused campaign, step by step.

1. **Plan.** Choose a workflow, the account, the budget in its currency, bidding, goals, landing page and creative.
   **Create paused campaign plan** sends nothing.
2. **Check.** Fix each blocking item, then choose **Check prerequisites again**.
3. **Approve.** Read every step of **Approve this complete plan** and choose **Save plan approval**. It covers this
   exact plan for 24 hours.
4. **Run.** **Run the approved plan** works on the server. Each step reads the account, prepares a review
   (**Prepare again** refreshes it), checks your limits, sends once and reads back (**Read-back evidence**).
   Everything is created paused.
5. **Turn it on.** Activation is a reviewed change of its own.

## Spending limits

In **Workspace settings → Account spending policy**, set caps per account, or a workspace default, in the account's
currency: budget, increase per change and per day, bid and total budget.

- An account without a policy is unprotected: save a workspace default first.
- Beyond a cap, AMCP asks for confirmation or refuses, as you choose; only the owner can approve it.
- Leave **Allow activation after review** and **Allow deletion after review** off unless you need them.
- Caps limit what AMCP requests, not what the platform bills.
