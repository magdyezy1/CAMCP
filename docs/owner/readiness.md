# Check that an account is ready

<a id="on-home"></a><a id="what-a-blocked-account-usually-needs"></a>

- On **Home**, **Account readiness** counts the accounts that are ready, blocked and still to check;
  **Accounts and their readiness** lists them.
- **Check account** reads an account now, read-only, and **Prerequisites** says why each check passed or failed.
  Check again before a launch.
- A blocked account usually needs **Reauthorize**, a selection in **Connections**, or a fix at the platform, such as
  billing. AMCP names what is missing; it never invents a value.

<a id="what-each-account-can-do"></a>

**Capabilities** shows what each account can use. On Home, *Capabilities and evidence* says what AMCP does for you;
**Technical details** keeps the tool names and evidence states. *Implemented, not live-verified* means tested, not yet
verified on a real account.
