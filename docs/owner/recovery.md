# When something needs attention

<a id="where-to-look"></a>

**Home** starts with what needs you. **Operations** lists stuck, uncertain or failed work with its next safe step,
**Monitoring inbox** holds your read-only alerts, and **Audit activity** records who did what.

## A change whose outcome is uncertain

After a timeout, a platform may not say whether a change was applied. AMCP marks it uncertain and does **not** send it
again, which could double a budget.

1. In **Operations**, choose **Check the provider (read-only)**.
2. If the platform holds the change, choose **Accept as applied**.
3. If not, prepare it again as a new review.

## Other cases

<a id="other-common-cases"></a>

- **A review expired**: nothing was sent. Choose **Prepare again**.
- **A login stopped working**: **Reauthorize** it.
- **An upload expired**: files from your computer last 30 minutes. Upload again.
- **A schedule paused** because its access changed: review it, then resume it.

## Emergency stop and help

<a id="the-emergency-stop"></a><a id="getting-help"></a>

**Workspace settings → Pause all writes** stops every reviewed change and upload until an owner resumes.
**Prepare support bundle** in **Operations** builds a sanitized bundle to inspect before you share it. Quote an error's
reference when you ask for help.
