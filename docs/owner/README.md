# AMCP owner guide

AMCP is your private advertising workspace: connect your clients' ad accounts, prepare campaigns, report results and
keep a record of every change, on the website or through an AI assistant. Members also find this guide under **Help**.

## How AMCP keeps you in control

<a id="words-used-in-this-guide"></a>

- **Nothing changes without a review.** AMCP reads the account, shows exactly what would change and waits for
  approval.
- **New campaigns start paused.** Turning one on is its own reviewed step.
- **Your limits apply.** Spending caps are checked before every change.
- **Every change is checked afterwards.** An unclear outcome is reported, never sent twice.
- **One switch stops everything:** **Workspace settings → Pause all writes**.
- **Numbers stay honest.** Missing data makes a report partial, never a guess or a zero.
