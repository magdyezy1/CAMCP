# The measurement hub

**Measurement** checks conversion tracking across your platforms, read-only: it never sends a test event or changes a
tag.

- **Measurement evidence ledger** shows how far the evidence goes for an account, property, pixel or app, layer by
  layer: *Observed*, *Unknown*, *Unavailable* or *Not applicable*.
- Other tasks read conversion goals and GA4, Google Tag Manager and LinkedIn settings.
  **Inspect a sanitized event trace** checks a trace on your computer without uploading it.

*Unknown* means not proven, not broken. Settings do not prove that conversions arrive: check the activity before a
campaign bids on a goal.
