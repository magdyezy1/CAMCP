# Reports and exports

Reports in AMCP keep every number as the platform reported it, with its currency, its reporting time zone and its
attribution. A report that is missing data says it is partial; AMCP never fills a gap with a guess or a zero.

## Build a report

1. Open **Report builder**.
2. Choose any combination of accounts (each keeps its own currency and time zone), the level (campaigns, ad sets or ad
   groups, or ads), the dates and the metrics.
3. Choose **Pull report**. The report is collected on the server: you can leave the page and come back to it.
4. Read the results: the totals, the rows, and where each number comes from. **Recommendations** are advice only;
   acting on one is a separate, reviewed change.
5. **Save report** keeps the definition, so you can pull it again or schedule it.

## Share and export

- **Download CSV** gives every row of the report.
- **Export now to Google Sheets** writes the report to a tab of a spreadsheet you choose. Connect Google Sheets in
  Connections first.
- **Sheets & schedules** delivers a saved report to Google Sheets on a schedule. Choose
  **Deliver complete reports only** or **Allow clearly marked partial reports**.
- **PDF and Excel**: a client report pack, with the client's approved branding, built from **Reporting**.

If AMCP cannot confirm that a delivery reached Google Sheets, it shows *Unconfirmed Google Sheets delivery*. After a
short wait you can resend the same report to that tab, or release the tab. AMCP never writes an older report over a
newer one.

## Saved reports, templates and schedules

- **Reporting** lists the saved report definitions, the templates and the retained snapshots (a snapshot is the fixed
  record of one pulled report), and builds client report packs.
- **Schedules** shows when each delivery runs next, in the delivery's time zone first, and lets you pause, resume or
  repair a schedule.
- **Evidence & analysis** keeps the detailed evidence behind reports and recommendations, with the platform's original
  rows.

## Who can download

Operators, administrators and owners download CSV, PDF, Excel and evidence files. Viewers read the same reports on
screen; the owner can let them download too, in **Workspace settings → Report file downloads**.
