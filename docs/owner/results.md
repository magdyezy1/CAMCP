# Reports and exports

1. In **Report builder**, choose accounts, level, dates and metrics, then **Pull report**.
2. Read the totals and their sources. **Recommendations** are advice; acting on one is a new review.
3. **Save report** keeps the definition to pull again or schedule.

## Share and deliver

- **Download CSV** gives every row; **Export now to Google Sheets** writes a tab.
- A schedule delivers a saved report to Google Sheets: choose **Deliver complete reports only** or
  **Allow clearly marked partial reports**.
- **Reporting** builds client report packs, **Schedules** shows the next deliveries, and **Evidence & analysis** keeps
  the evidence behind each number.

*Unconfirmed Google Sheets delivery*: after a short wait, resend it or release the tab. An older report never
overwrites a newer one. Viewers download files only if the owner allows it in
**Workspace settings → Report file downloads**.
