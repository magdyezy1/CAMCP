Disconnect this login?

AMCP will remove its local credentials, asset selections and the report data read through this login. Saved report definitions and the change history stay. Your advertising account will not be changed.

Google ends AMCP’s access for this Google account as a whole. The same person’s other AMCP Google logins (Google Ads, Analytics, Tag Manager, Search Console, DV360, Business Profile, Sheets and Drive), in any workspace, may stop working and need to be connected again.
