1. About these terms

In these terms, “we” means the operator of AMCP at amcp.shop, and “you” means the person using it and the organization they act for.

If you use AMCP on behalf of an organization, you confirm that you may accept these terms for it.

2. Accounts and access

  • Who can join: AMCP accounts are created by invitation or, where a deployment opens registration, through verified sign-up. A workspace owner approves new members and assigns their role.
  • Your credentials: keep your password, recovery codes and connector tokens private. Use two-step verification where it is offered. You are responsible for activity under your account until you tell us or your workspace owner that it is compromised.
  • Workspaces: the workspace owner decides who can see the workspace, which connected accounts it uses and which of them may accept changes.

3. Connected platforms

Connect only accounts, properties and data that you are authorized to access and manage. Each platform's own terms, policies, approvals and access levels continue to apply, and the platform decides whether an account is eligible for a feature. AMCP shows what it can read or prepare; it does not change what a platform allows.

You can disconnect a login at any time. You can also remove AMCP's access in the platform's own account settings.

4. Advertising changes

A deployment runs in one of two modes, shown in the workspace as “Read-only workspace” or “Read + reviewed writes”.

  • Read-only: AMCP reads data and prepares local previews. It does not change your advertising accounts.
  • Reviewed writes: AMCP can change only accounts that the workspace owner explicitly enables for writes. Each change is prepared, shown for review and runs only after someone with the right access confirms it. Owner spending policies and confirmation steps apply.

You are responsible for the changes you confirm, including their effect on campaign delivery and spend. Advertising charges are between you and the platform. Recommendations and reports are decision support, not a guarantee of results.

5. AI tools and connector tokens

When you connect an AI tool, it can use AMCP within the access you grant: reading your selected accounts and, where enabled, preparing reviewed changes. Connector tokens expire after 30 days and can be revoked in MCP connectors. The AI tool's own terms and privacy policy govern how it processes what it receives.
